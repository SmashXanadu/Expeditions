# Hex Sticker Prompts

Prompts used to generate the location hex art (ChatGPT/Gemini). Final sticker size is 1.05" x 1.21" (315x363 px at 300 dpi), pointy-top hex. The art is generated as a plain square with no text, then cropped to the hex, bordered, and labeled by script.

## How to make a new one

1. Start a NEW chat and paste the Style Block first. Wait for "ready".
2. Paste one location prompt at a time, in the same chat, so the style stays consistent.
3. If text, a border, or a frame appears, ask it to regenerate with none.
4. Save the result in this folder named after the location.

New location prompt template:

```
[Location name]: [what the place physically is, 1 sentence]. [1 to 2 key landmarks or details to show]. [Colors / natural features]. [Mood] mood.
```

Keep it to one clear focal landmark and one accent color.

## Style Block (paste first)

```
I'm making a set of small icon illustrations for hex stickers on a pirate-age fantasy sea map. Use the same style for every image I ask for in this chat:
- Flat, simple hand-inked illustration with soft watercolor wash, like an antique nautical chart vignette
- Warm parchment background (cream, #F1E4C8), ink lines in dark brown, limited palette with one accent color per image
- ONE clear focal landmark centered in the frame, simple bold shapes that stay readable when printed at 1 inch wide
- Minimal detail, no fine texture, no tiny elements, plenty of empty space around the subject
- Slight top-down three-quarter view, with a little sea or ground at the base
- Square 1:1 image, subject centered with a safe margin on all sides
- ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, labels, banners, borders, or frames anywhere in the image
Reply "ready" and wait for my first location.
```

## Location Prompts

### Weirwild Atoll (accent: bioluminescent teal)
```
Weirwild Atoll: a colossal ancient forest growing out of a shallow reef, giant roots knotted together above and below the waterline. A small village of rope-lashed wooden platforms and bridges is suspended in the upper branches. Dense dark-green canopy, glowing teal-blue bioluminescent pools and growths among the roots, a tiny canoe in the water at the base. Primal and mysterious mood.
```

### Sunbloom Key (accent: fresh green and sun yellow)
```
Sunbloom Key: a tiny ring of sun-bleached stone just above the waves, with a circular dock-town of silos and drying racks on the rock. Surrounding it are many small floating farm platforms tethered by ropes, each with a green crop canopy, forming a patchwork on blue water. A small skiff with a farmer. Gentle, hopeful, sunny mood.
```

### Rustreach Crag (accent: rust orange and copper green)
```
Rustreach Crag: a steep jagged rock rising from the sea like a broken tooth. Its face is riddled with dark vertical mine shafts, with a lattice of industrial wooden balconies, cranes, and hanging walkways clinging to the cliff. Stains of orange rust and green copper oxidation down the stone, a thin plume of smelter smoke at the top. Industrial, vertical, and metallic mood.
```

### Siren's Rest (accent: turquoise and blush pink)
```
Siren's Rest: a tropical paradise island with white sand and a turquoise lagoon. In the center, a tall pink-marbled monolith with a visible hairline crack, surrounded by crumbling pink marble ruins. A large open-air pavilion with billowing silk awnings by the shore, palm trees. Serene and luxurious on the surface, with a faint tension to it.
```

### Ashfall Peak (accent: ember orange against black)
```
Ashfall Peak: a volcanic island with black sand beaches and a large slumbering volcano at its heart, a thin wisp of smoke and a faint ember glow at the crater. Lush green jungle on the lower slopes, jagged obsidian ridges higher up. A cluster of stilted wooden longhouses on the black sand with a few glowing forge fires. Fertile, adventurous frontier mood.
```

### The Driftworks (accent: iron grey and warm lantern yellow)
```
The Driftworks: a ramshackle floating scrap-city in the spirit of Waterworld, welded and lashed together from salvaged hulls, rusted barges, oil drums, and mismatched planks. Patchwork shacks and lean-to huts stacked haphazardly on top, rope bridges and rickety gangplanks between uneven decks, tarps and sails stretched as roofs, a crooked scrap-metal crane and a central iron-clad hull as the core. Salvage heaps, hanging nets, and laundry lines, small dinghies tied alongside, strings of lanterns glowing warm yellow. Everything looks patched, tilted, and improvised, nothing symmetrical. Crowded, scrappy, resourceful mood.
```

The Emerald Dawn: a single sturdy exploration sailing ship with emerald-green painted hull trim and sails with a warm cream hull, seen from a slight three-quarter angle on calm blue water. Looks like a well-kept long-distance vessel, not a warship, with a lookout crow's nest and a small glowing blue stone at the bow. Adventurous and hopeful mood.
