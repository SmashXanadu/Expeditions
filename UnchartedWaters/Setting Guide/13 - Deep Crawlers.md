# [Deep Crawlers](#deep-crawlers)

*Deep Sea Scavengers*

The Deep Crawlers treat the ocean floor as an unplundered museum, and they're the primary source of Old World technology and metals anywhere on the sea. You can spot one in any port by the scars, the pressure-burns, and the occasional brass augmentation bolted where something used to be. Whatever the old civilization left behind down there, the Crawlers are the ones bringing it up, and they're not in the habit of asking whether it should stay buried.

- **Home Port:** Rustreach Crag (Ch. 7), where they run the Abyssal Cradle at the Crag's submerged base.
- **Allies:** The Horizon Chasers. Kindred spirits, one exploring below the waterline and one above it. They trade charts, salvage rights, and rumors freely.
- **Rivals:** The Tidal Conclave (who believe every Old World relic belongs in their observatories) and the Copper Coalition (who share the Fang and suspect the Crawlers of skimming their shafts).
- **What They Want From Your Crew:** Extra bodies on the tether line, and buyers for what comes up. The Crawlers pay in salvage rights: first pick of a wreck, a cut of the haul, or a relic nobody else could identify.

## Foreman Grogori

- **Role:** Foreman of the Deep Crawlers and master of the Azure Abyss.
- **Behavior:** Gruff, patient, and impossible to rush. Talks slowly between whistling breaths and expects the same calm from everyone else on the line. Panic gets divers killed.
- **Quirk:** Spends more time in his custom brass diving suit than out of it, and hasn't fully taken it off in months. His lungs whistle from years of breathing pressurized air, and he raps twice on his own helmet before every dive, for luck.
- **Drive:** Recover what the Old World left behind before the silt swallows it forever. Grogori believes the old civilization knew something about the Cataclysm, and that the answer is sitting on the seabed waiting for someone brave enough to read it.
- **Hook:** Sunken vaults, drowned ruins, Old World devices, and salvage races against rival crews. If something strange was pulled up from the deep, a Crawler either found it or knows who did.

## The Azure Abyss

A low, sturdy salvage vessel bristling with cranes, heavy winches, and diving bells. Its double-reinforced hull can sit over the deepest trenches without buckling, and its floodlights cut through the lightless dark of the seabed like knives.

- **Aboard:** A maze of pipes, pressure gauges, and steam-valves. A **decompression chamber**, a workshop for repairing salvaged technology, and the **Wet Deck**, where divers suit up and drop through the hull into open water.
- **Zale:** Lead Salvage Specialist. Can identify a valuable relic through an inch of silt and rust, and will argue its price before anyone's even sure what it does.
- **Dottie:** Engineer and crane operator. Runs every winch, cable, and bell that lowers divers into the dark, and has never lost anyone on her line. She means to keep it that way.
