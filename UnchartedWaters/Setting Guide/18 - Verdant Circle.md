# [Verdant Circle](#verdant-circle)

*Druids*

The Verdant Circle sees the ocean as a single living organism, and themselves as its immune system. They travel in grown ships, use spirit-magic to turn sea life and currents against anyone who pollutes the deep, and they don't much care whose trade route gets in the way. To the Circle, "dead-water" is a wound, and the people who cause it are the infection.

- **Home Port:** Weirwild Atoll (Ch. 10), where they tend the shadowed pools and keep the canopy a sanctuary for the sea's oldest spirits.
- **Allies:** The Tidal Conclave, cautiously, and only when the threat is big enough. The Black Fleet shares their enemies, and the two leave each other alone.
- **Rivals:** The Copper Coalition (whose mining the Circle calls a slow murder of the ocean floor, and whose ships it raids near Rustreach Crag) and the Horizon Chasers (whose every new chart is an invitation for someone to exploit what the Circle protects).
- **What They Want From Your Crew:** Outsiders who can go where a druid would be shot on sight. The Circle pays in safe passage through its waters, medicine nobody else can grow, and the goodwill of things in the deep that remember who helped them.

## Arch-Druid Thalassa

- **Role:** Arch-Druid of the Verdant Circle and the living heart of the Verdant Wake.
- **Behavior:** Calm, slow, and ancient-feeling, like talking to a tree that's decided to listen. Speaks of the sea as "she" and of most people as visitors. Patient right up until she isn't.
- **Quirk:** Her skin has begun to take on the texture of polished driftwood, spreading a little further every year. She doesn't hide it, and doesn't explain it.
- **Drive:** Purge the dead-water and protect the endangered leviathans before the sea's oldest spirits are gone for good. Thalassa believes something vast sleeps in the deep, and that the Coalition's digging is waking it the wrong way.
- **Hook:** Pollution, endangered creatures, ecological mysteries, and blockades. Pip's search for a cure and Finn's sleeping sea spirit both lead straight to her door.

## The Verdant Wake

A living vessel grown rather than built. Its hull is shaped from enchanted ironwood braided with living coral and reefstone, its ribs are wrapped in vines, and its sails are woven from kelp-silk that flexes and breathes. Fish gather in its shadow wherever it goes, and the sea around it turns strangely calm, or violently hostile, depending on the Circle's will.

- **Aboard:** A drifting ecosystem. The **Heart-Grove**, a small forest grown around the central mast, serves as council chamber and ritual space. Hollowed root-chambers below hold water, seeds, and medicinal growths instead of cargo, and the crew sleeps in grown cocoons of bark and vine that hum faintly with the ship's pulse.
- **Rootwarden Mael:** Caretaker and defender. Tends the living hull, directs coral reinforcement, and wakes the ship's natural defenses when it's threatened. Treats the Wake like a large, temperamental animal, because it is one.
- **Spore-Seer Nysa:** Navigator. Reads tides, winds, and whale-song through ritual spores and living charts grown from algae and bark. Getting directions from Nysa takes a while and usually involves inhaling something.
