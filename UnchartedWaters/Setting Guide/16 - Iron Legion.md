# [Iron Legion](#iron-legion)

*Mercenaries*

The Iron Legion is a disciplined military corporation that sells elite protection and naval enforcement to whoever can afford it. Their heavy warships favor tactical superiority and overwhelming firepower, and their marines train for boarding actions the way other crews train for storms. The Legion doesn't pick sides. It picks contracts, and right now, the biggest contract on the sea belongs to the Gilded Union.

- **Home Port:** Ashfall Peak (Ch. 5), where a rotating garrison keeps the Ridge Watchtowers and watches the horizon for miles. The Legion goes wherever the contract is, but Ashfall is where it's most visible.
- **Allies:** The Gilded Union. Contracted, well paid, and loyal, for now.
- **Rivals:** The Black Fleet (constant skirmishes, since every Legion captain on Union coin is a traitor in Vane's eyes) and the Horizon Chasers (whose charts the Legion badly wants, and Elara knows it).
- **What They Want From Your Crew:** Scouts, guides, and specialists the Legion can afford to lose. They pay exactly what was agreed, exactly on time. Read the contract before signing it.

## Commander Valerius

- **Role:** Commander of the Iron Legion and master of the Crimson Tide.
- **Behavior:** Cold, calculating, and economical with words. Treats naval warfare like a game of chess, and treats people a little like pieces. Demands absolute discipline and returns absolute loyalty to anyone who gives it.
- **Quirk:** Plays chess against himself in the war room every night, always the same opening, and has never been seen to lose. His scars are mapped like a campaign history, and he can tell you which battle gave him each one.
- **Drive:** Fulfill every contract with total efficiency. A broken contract is the only defeat Valerius acknowledges. Quietly, he's also positioning the Legion so that when the Union's contract ends, every other faction will need to bid for it.
- **Hook:** Escort jobs, monster hunts, bounties, and expeditions that need a scout who can be spared. Also any job where the party might be asked to choose between the contract and doing the right thing.

## The Crimson Tide

A heavily armored war-galley with a hull painted deep, intimidating red. A reinforced ram splinters enemy timber, multiple decks of heavy ballistae stand ready, and the oars beat with a rhythmic, terrifying precision that tells every crew in earshot exactly who is coming.

- **Aboard:** A floating fortress. An extensive armory, a **tactical war room** for planning engagements, reinforced battle stations, and a sterile, efficient **Med-Ward** built to put wounded marines back on deck fast.
- **Kaelen:** Master-at-Arms. A specialist in ship-to-ship boarding tactics who trains the Legion's marine detachment. Respects anyone who can hold a deck under pressure, whichever side they're on.
- **Sarge:** The grizzled bosun. Makes sure the heavy weapons are always primed, and has an opinion about everyone who sets foot on deck. He's usually right.
