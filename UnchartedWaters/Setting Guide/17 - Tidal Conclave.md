# [Tidal Conclave](#tidal-conclave)

*Mages*

The Tidal Conclave is an elitist society of scholars and mystics who draw their power from the celestial bodies and the pull of the tides. They keep to their crystalline observatories and rarely deal with the rest of the sea, emerging only when the cosmic balance is threatened. Lately, with the moon misbehaving and the Siren-Stone cracking, they've been emerging a lot.

- **Home Port:** Siren's Rest (Ch. 8), where they keep the pink-marbled ruins and are the only ones who understand how to maintain the Peace-Bond.
- **Allies:** The Verdant Circle. A cautious alliance against unchecked exploitation of natural forces. Neither trusts the other's methods.
- **Rivals:** The Deep Crawlers (who keep hauling up Old World relics the Conclave believes belong in its observatories) and the Gilded Union (who want the Conclave's tide-charts, and who Selene is sure would weaponize them).
- **What They Want From Your Crew:** Hands and eyes outside the observatory walls. The Conclave pays in knowledge: a reading of the stars, a translated inscription, or the answer to a question nobody else can.

## High Oracle Selene

- **Role:** High Oracle of the Tidal Conclave and master of the Silver Crescent.
- **Behavior:** Serene, unhurried, and maddeningly cryptic. Blind since birth, she navigates purely by the pull of the tides and the alignment of the stars. Speaks in riddles, but her navigation is never wrong.
- **Quirk:** Always knows the exact state of the tide, anywhere, without asking. She'll pause mid-sentence, tilt her head, and announce that the water just turned.
- **Drive:** Understand the celestial alignments well enough to harness the moon's power over the tides and currents. Selene believes the Cataclysm left the sky and the sea out of balance, and that the blood-red moon is the first sign of it getting worse.
- **Hook:** Celestial events, strange tides, magical ruins, and anything involving the Peace-Bond. Captain Wren's star-chart hunt makes the Conclave a natural ally, or a dangerous rival.

## The Silver Crescent

An elegant, slender ship of white marble-infused timber and silvered wood. It leaves a glowing wake of moonlight behind it, its sails catch wind even when the air is still, and its hull hums with a faint, melodic resonance that matches the tides.

- **Aboard:** A massive **astronomical observatory**, an alchemical laboratory, and a meditation garden. The quarters are lit by captured moonlight and kept so quiet you can hear the hull singing.
- **Kael:** Astral Navigator. Maintains the arcane instruments and crystal lenses that track the moon's pull on the water, and translates Selene's riddles into headings the crew can actually steer by.
- **Lyra:** Spell-Weaver. Runs the ritual magic that keeps the lunar-sails filled and the marble hull safe from salt. Quietly the most powerful mage aboard, and doesn't want anyone to know it.
