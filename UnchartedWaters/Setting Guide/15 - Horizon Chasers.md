# [Horizon Chasers](#horizon-chasers)

*Explorers*

The Horizon Chasers are a fellowship of voyagers who believe the ocean has no end, only undiscovered beginnings. Everyone else in the sea treats the Great Fog as a wall. The Chasers treat it as a coastline nobody has charted yet. In the meantime, they're comparing every old chart they can find against what's actually out there, and filling in the blank spots one reef and one current at a time.

- **Home Port:** The Driftworks (Ch. 6), where they keep a standing chart-room aboard one of the hulls. Since the armada drifts, every month's expedition launches from a new coordinate.
- **Allies:** The Deep Crawlers. Same hunger for the unknown, one above the waterline and one below it.
- **Rivals:** The Iron Legion (who want Elara's charts, since a complete map of hidden routes is worth a fortune in patrol contracts) and the Verdant Circle (who see every new chart as an invitation for someone to exploit what they protect).
- **What They Want From Your Crew:** Eyes on places the Cobalt Needle can't reach this season. The Chasers pay in charts, safe routes, and the honor of having a reef named after you.

## Chief Cartographer Elara

- **Role:** Chief Cartographer of the Horizon Chasers and master of the Cobalt Needle.
- **Behavior:** Bright, restless, and talks with her hands, usually while holding a pen. Values discovery over gold or blood, and gets visibly bored when a conversation turns to either.
- **Quirk:** Keeps a pocket chart of the whole sea, with every blank spot inked over in a different color as it's filled. She checks it the way other people check the weather.
- **Drive:** Prove there's something past the Fog. Elara has found evidence of a **Hidden Continent** and needs a crew willing to follow it past the edge of every map. She also knows the Legion wants her charts, and she's been very careful about who sees them.
- **Hook:** Unmapped islands, shifting currents, old charts that don't match what's there anymore, and any expedition that ends with "nobody's ever been here before."

## The Cobalt Needle

A small, nimble scout-ship crammed with telescopes, drafting tables, and strange measuring instruments. Built for long-range endurance, with a reinforced bow for pushing through unknown debris and a deep-blue hull that blends into open water.

- **Aboard:** Essentially a floating library and laboratory. A huge **chart room**, an archive of ancient scrolls, and instruments for measuring ocean depths and current speeds.
- **Milo:** The Cartographer's Apprentice. Lives at the drafting table, updating charts with every new reef and island, and gets very upset when someone spills on them.
- **Tracker:** The ship's scout. Usually perched in the crow's nest or out in a rowboat, and always the first to set foot on newly discovered land. Doesn't say much. Never seems to need to.
