# [Gilded Union](#gilded-union)

*Traders*

The Gilded Union is a massive, bureaucratic network of trade lords who believe gold is the only true law. They control the flow of spice, fresh water, food, and luxury across the sea, and they use that wealth to buy politics, protection, and silence. The Union rarely breaks a rule. It writes the rules, then charges everyone else to follow them.

- **Home Port:** Sunbloom Key (Ch. 9), where the Union holds exclusive contracts on the harvest. Not ownership, but close enough.
- **Allies:** The Iron Legion (contracted muscle, well paid and loyal for now) and the Copper Coalition (who need the Union's network to sell their ore).
- **Rivals:** The Black Fleet (the sea's defining war, with a standing bounty on Vane's head) and the Tidal Conclave (whose tide-charts Aurelia has offered a fortune for, and been refused every time).
- **What They Want From Your Crew:** Discretion. The Union hires outsiders for anything it can't be seen doing itself. It pays well, on time, and in writing, and every contract has a clause nobody reads until it matters.

## High Factor Aurelia

- **Role:** High Factor of the Gilded Union and master of the Golden Sovereign.
- **Behavior:** Gracious, warm, and always three negotiations ahead. Values a silver tongue as much as a sharp sword, and is very good with both. Never threatens anyone. Simply explains, kindly, what will happen.
- **Quirk:** Never carries coin. Everything she buys goes on a note signed in gold ink, and every merchant in the sea honors it without question.
- **Drive:** A monopoly on every profitable lane in the sea, and a world stable enough to keep it. Aurelia sees the Black Fleet as the last real threat to that order and Vane as a personal insult. The tide-charts are next.
- **Hook:** Investigations, contract disputes, escort jobs, and quiet favors with very generous payment. Anything involving the Fleet, the phantom ship, or a rival's secrets.

## The Golden Sovereign

A massive, four-masted treasure galleon with gold-leaf detailing and silk sails. More floating palace than cargo ship, built to carry the sea's most valuable luxuries and to remind everyone who sees it exactly who owns them.

- **Aboard:** Opulent staterooms, a grand dining hall, a wine cellar, and a garden terrace for the Union's high officials. At the center of the ship sits a **high-security vault** that takes three keys and Master Silas to open.
- **Master Silas:** Chief Purser and Accountant. Tracks every ounce of spice and every coin aboard, and notices when one goes missing. He's quietly convinced someone in the crew is feeding manifests to the Black Fleet.
- **Chef Remy:** Runs the Sovereign's legendary galley so guests and officers eat like royalty in the middle of the ocean. Hears everything said over dinner, and remembers it.
