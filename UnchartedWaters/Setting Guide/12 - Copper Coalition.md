# [Copper Coalition](#copper-coalition)

*Miners*

The Copper Coalition digs, smelts, and ships the raw metal every navy on the sea is built from. They live in heat and smoke on top of volatile vents, driven by a brutal work ethic and a simple belief: the ore doesn't care how tired you are. Nobody loves the Coalition, but every faction with a ship or a sword owes it something, and Overseer Bram never forgets a debt.

- **Home Port:** Rustreach Crag (Ch. 7), where the Coalition owns the vertical mine shafts and has turned the Fang into a factory.
- **Allies:** The Gilded Union. The Coalition's ore is worthless without a buyer, and the Union is the only network big enough to move it. Neither side loves the arrangement.
- **Rivals:** The Verdant Circle (who call the Coalition's mining a slow murder of the ocean floor, and whose raids near the Crag are escalating) and the Deep Crawlers (who share the Fang, and who Bram is sure are skimming his ore).
- **What They Want From Your Crew:** Hands that don't belong to the company. The Coalition hires outsiders for jobs too dangerous, too dirty, or too deniable for its own miners, and it pays promptly, in coin or in metal.

## Overseer Bram

- **Role:** Company-appointed Overseer of Rustreach Crag and master of the Copper Ember.
- **Behavior:** Soot-covered and blunt, talks in numbers and deadlines. Doesn't raise his voice, just keeps repeating the quota until people stop arguing.
- **Quirk:** Carries a battered tally-board everywhere and chalks a mark on it for every load that comes up the lift. Has never been seen to wipe it clean.
- **Drive:** Make quota, every time. Bram genuinely believes the sea runs on Coalition metal and that one missed shipment means someone, somewhere, sails out in a ship that isn't strong enough. Safety is a cost. Shortfalls are a catastrophe.
- **Hook:** Industrial disasters, sabotage, flooded shafts, vent eruptions, and anything that threatens the next shipment, especially if the Verdant Circle or the Deep Crawlers might be behind it.

## The Copper Ember

A steam-belching, iron-clad ore hauler that's always hot to the touch and always smells of sulfur and coal. Massive furnaces and cooling vents glow orange through the night, and a pair of thundering paddle-wheels drive it through the water where other ships trust the wind.

- **Aboard:** A working **foundry** that refines minerals on the move, a high-heat forge, and a cavernous ore hold. The bunks sit directly above the boilers to save cargo space, and nobody aboard ever sleeps well.
- **Hammer:** Lead Blacksmith. Runs the onboard forge and can patch a breached hull or cast fresh cannonballs mid-voyage. Speaks mostly in grunts and is very good at the job.
- **Steam-Eye Joe:** Head Engineer. Keeps the volatile boilers from tearing the ship apart, and lost one eye to a pressure valve proving it. Loves the Ember the way other sailors love the sea.
