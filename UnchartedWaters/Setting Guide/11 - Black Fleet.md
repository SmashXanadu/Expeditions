# [Black Fleet](#black-fleet)

*Pirates*

The Black Fleet is less a navy and more a ghost story. They strike through misdirection, disguise, and a web of informants planted in the Gilded Union's own shipping crews, then vanish before anyone can say which way they went. They don't consider themselves criminals. As far as the Fleet is concerned, they're the only honest people on the water, because they're the only ones not pretending the sea belongs to somebody.

- **Home Port:** The Driftworks (Ch. 6), where the ever-shifting tangle of hulls hides their ships and fences their cargo.
- **Allies:** None, officially. The Verdant Circle shares their enemies and keeps a wary, respectful distance.
- **Rivals:** The Gilded Union (the sea's defining war: Aurelia has a bounty on Vane's head, and Vane has sunk three Union flagships in return) and the Iron Legion (any captain who takes Union coin is a traitor to the sea).
- **What They Want From Your Crew:** Eyes and hands in places the Fleet can't go openly. They pay in shares, not wages, and they remember favors for a very long time. So do the people they rob.

## Captain Vane the Shadow

- **Role:** Captain of the Black Talon and the closest thing the Fleet has to a leader.
- **Behavior:** Unhurried and soft-spoken, with a low, amused voice that never needs to rise. Prefers to be heard before he's seen, and is rarely seen at all.
- **Quirk:** Carries a shuttered black lantern whose flame burns *black*. Opened, it spills a heavy, sweet-smelling fog that puts a whole deck to sleep. It's how the Talon takes ships without firing a single cannon, and Vane treats it like an old friend.
- **Drive:** Break the Gilded Union's grip on the shipping lanes, and with it the idea that anyone can own open water. Vane would rather humiliate the Union than bleed it. Dead sailors make martyrs. Sleeping ones make jokes.
- **Hook:** Heists, ransoms, smuggling runs, and anything that embarrasses the Union. If your players need something moved, hidden, or made to disappear, someone in the Fleet knows a way.

## The Black Talon

A sleek, black-sailed schooner built for speed and stealth. It sits so low in the water it's nearly invisible at night or in heavy fog, and it's been stripped of every ounce of excess weight so it can close distance and board before its prey can react.

- **Aboard:** Lean and cramped. A hidden **Smuggler's Hold** for stolen goods, a fast-access armory, and hammocks slung between the cannons because there's nowhere else to sleep.
- **Scarlet:** Quartermaster. Polite, orderly, and absolutely never bluffing. Keeps the ship's ledger on a cord around her neck and divides every haul strictly by the pirate code. If the Fleet sends someone to talk to your players first, it's Scarlet.
- **Ratty:** Lookout and rigger. Small, nimble, and fidgety, and can spot a merchant sail on the horizon miles before anyone else. Ratty also makes an excellent spy, mostly because nobody ever looks twice at Ratty.
