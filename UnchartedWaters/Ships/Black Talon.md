### The Black Talon

*Flagship of [The Black Fleet]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/Factions/TheBlackFleet/#the-black-fleet)*

A sleek, black-sailed schooner that sits so low in the water it's nearly invisible at night or in fog. Every ounce of excess weight has been stripped out so she can close distance and board before her prey can fire a shot. Most ships she takes never hear a cannon. They just wake up lighter.

- **The Vibe:** Lean, quiet, fast. A ghost story with a crew.
- **Finding Her:** You don't. She finds you, usually out of a fog bank.
- **Home Port:** Hidden somewhere in the tangle of the Driftworks

**Senses:**
- **Looks:** Black wood, black canvas, nothing painted, nothing polished. No lights showing after dark.
- **Sounds:** Water hissing fast along the hull. Low voices. Whistled signals instead of shouted orders.
- **Smells:** Pitch, gunpowder, bilge, and a faint sweetness from the captain's lantern
- **Feels:** Cramped and quick. The deck heels hard and never settles.

---------
### Layout

Four decks. A single stairway climbs from the hold to the crew deck and another to the officers' deck. Four stairways (two aft, two forward) climb from there to the main deck.

- **Main Deck:** Three masts, the wheel on a raised quarterdeck aft, a raised forecastle forward, a grated hatch amidships. Boats hung over the side. Ratty somewhere up in the rigging.
- **Officers' Deck:** The **Captain's Cabin** (stern) is Vane's. Small, dark, and quiet. The lantern hangs here when it isn't working. Beside it, the **Wardroom**: a long table, six chairs, charts pinned down with knives. **The Armory** is racked at the foot of the stairs amidships so the crew can arm and board fast. A small forward cabin for off-watch hands.
- **Crew Deck:** **Galley** at the bow. Two mess tables around an open cargo hatch. Curtained bunks along both hulls. Aft, a rum storeroom and **Scarlet's Office**: a desk, ink, and every old ledger the Fleet has kept.
- **The Hold:** Crates and barrels packed bow and stern, hammocks slung along both hulls. **The Brig** is an iron-barred cell down here, with a lantern hook outside the bars and one guard on a stool. The **Smuggler's Hold** hides under a false floor: stolen goods and confiscated gear, all logged.

------------
### Occupants

- **[Captain Vane the Shadow]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/NPCs/Vane/#captain-vane-the-shadow):** Heard before he's seen. Carries the black lantern.
- **[Scarlet]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/NPCs/Scarlet/#scarlet):** Quartermaster. Runs the ship day to day. Ledger on a cord.
- **Ratty:** Lookout, rigger, spy. Small, fidgety, bored. Nobody looks twice at Ratty.
- **The Crew:** A dozen or so deckhands. Loyal to the code and to their shares.

------------
### House Quirks

- Nothing comes aboard until Scarlet writes it down. Nothing leaves either.
- Nobody touches the captain's lantern. Not even to light it.
- The Fleet doesn't kill. Prisoners wake up adrift in a rowboat, broke and embarrassed.
