# [The Black Fleet](#the-black-fleet)
*Pirates*

- **What they are:** Less a navy, more a ghost story. Misdirection, disguise, and informants inside Union shipping crews. Strike without warning, vanish without a trace.
- **How they see themselves:** Not criminals. The only honest people on the water, because nobody can own the sea.
- **Ship:** [The Black Talon]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/Locations/Ships/TheBlackTalon/#the-black-talon). Sleek, black-sailed schooner, sits low, nearly invisible at night or in fog.
- **Home:** **The Driftworks.** Ships hidden in the tangle, stolen goods fenced through the markets. The Council pretends not to notice.
- **Motivation:** Break the Gilded Union's grip on the ocean. Live free of any law or king.
- **Pays in:** Shares, not wages. They remember favors for a long time. So do the people they rob.

---

### Key Crew

- **Captain Vane the Shadow** (he/him): Boards ships without firing a cannon. Unhurried, low amused voice, rarely seen. Carries a **black lantern** whose black flame spills sleeping fog. Would rather humiliate the Union than bleed it.
- **Scarlet** (she/her), Quartermaster: Polite, orderly, never bluffs. Ledger on a cord around her neck. Splits every haul by the pirate code. Usually the Fleet's first face.
- **Ratty**, Lookout and Rigger: Small, nimble, fidgety. Spots sails miles out. Makes a great spy because nobody looks twice.

### Friends & Enemies

- **Gilded Union:** Enemies. Aurelia has a bounty on Vane's head; Vane has sunk three Union flagships.
- **Iron Legion:** Hostile. Any captain on Union coin is a traitor to the sea.
- **Verdant Circle:** Wary respect. Shared enemies, but no part of piracy.

### Hook

- The Black Talon has captured a royal envoy. The party is hired to rescue the envoy or negotiate a ransom before the Talon disappears.
