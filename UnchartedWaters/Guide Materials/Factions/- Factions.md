## [Faction Tensions](#faction-tensions)

| Factions | Relationship | Notes |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Gilded Union** ↔ **Black Fleet** | Enemies | Aurelia has a standing bounty on Vane's head. Vane has sunk three Union flagships in retaliation. This is the ocean's defining political conflict. |
| **Iron Legion** ↔ **Gilded Union** | Allied (contracted) | The Legion serves as the Union's naval enforcement arm. Well-paid and loyal — for now. |
| **Black Fleet** ↔ **Iron Legion** | Hostile | Any Legion captain who takes Union coin is, in Vane's view, a traitor to the sea. Skirmishes are common. |
| **Verdant Circle** ↔ **Copper Coalition** | Hostile | The Circle considers the Coalition's volcanic extraction a slow murder of the ocean floor. Raids near Rustreach Crag are escalating. |
| **Deep Crawlers** ↔ **Tidal Conclave** | Tense rivalry | Both claim authority over Old World relics. The Crawlers recover them; the Mages believe they belong in their observatories. No open war yet. |
| **Horizon Chasers** ↔ **Iron Legion** | Uneasy | The Legion wants Elara's charts — complete maps of hidden routes are worth a fortune to whoever controls the contracts to patrol them. Elara knows it. |
| **Tidal Conclave** ↔ **Verdant Circle** | Cautious alliance | Both oppose unchecked exploitation of natural forces, but distrust each other's methods. They cooperate only when the threat is large enough. |
| **Copper Coalition** ↔ **Gilded Union** | Allied (trade) | The Coalition's ore and alloys are worthless without a buyer, and the Union is the only network big enough to move them at scale. Neither side loves the arrangement, but both need it. |
| **Deep Crawlers** ↔ **Horizon Chasers** | Allied (kindred spirits) | Two factions built on the same hunger to find what nobody's found yet, one below the waterline and one above it. They trade charts, salvage rights, and rumors freely. |
| **Copper Coalition** ↔ **Deep Crawlers** | Uneasy rivals | They share the Fang at Rustreach Crag and the ore quotas that come with it. Bram suspects the Crawlers of skimming shafts that belong to the Coalition; both sides are quietly racing each other toward the rumored Living Copper below. |
| **Horizon Chasers** ↔ **Verdant Circle** | Tense | Elara's charts open routes that the Circle would rather stayed closed. Every new map is, to the Circle, an invitation for someone else to exploit what it protects. |
| **Gilded Union** ↔ **Tidal Conclave** | Distrustful | Aurelia wants the Conclave's tide-charts for her shipping lanes and has offered a fortune for them. Selene has refused every offer, certain the Union would weaponize the tides the moment it understood them. |
| **Black Fleet** ↔ **Verdant Circle** | Wary respect | Vane and the Circle share more enemies than friends, namely the Union and the Coalition, but the Circle wants no part of piracy and keeps its distance. Neither interferes with the other. |
