### **Captain Vane the Shadow**

\cols

![Vane]({{site.baseurl}}/images/NPCs/Vane.jpg "maxheight:210")

\col

**Ship:** [The Black Talon]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/Locations/Ships/TheBlackTalon/#the-black-talon)
**Ship Role:** Captain
**Faction:** [The Black Fleet]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/Factions/TheBlackFleet/#the-black-fleet)
**Pronouns:** he/him

**General Appearance:**
- Tall, unhurried. A long coat that seems to drink the light.
- A distinctive **scar over one eye**. That eye is **dead and foggy**.
- Rarely seen clearly. Fog tends to gather around his face.

**Sounds Like:**
- Soft-spoken. A low, amused voice that never needs to rise.
- Prefers to be heard before he's seen. Unhurried footsteps on the deck above.
- Speaks to enemies like a host apologizing for the inconvenience

\endcols

**Defining Characteristic(s):**
- Carries a shuttered **black lantern**. Its flame burns black and spills a heavy, sweet-smelling fog that puts a whole room to sleep.
- Boards ships without firing a cannon. Less a pirate than a ghost story.
- Captain's dagger engraved **"The Black Talon"**, after his ship. Only the captain carries one.

**Motivation:**
- Break the Gilded Union's grip on the sea, and the idea that anyone can own open water
- Would rather humiliate the Union than bleed it. Dead sailors make martyrs. Sleeping ones make jokes.

**Flaw or Quirk:**
- Treats the lantern like an old friend. Talks to it. Shields it from the wind.
- Bored by anything easy. Lets a game run longer than he should to see how it ends.

**With the Party:**
- The Fleet doesn't kill. Prisoners wake up adrift, broke, and embarrassed.
- Leaves the talking to Scarlet. Steps in only when things get *difficult*.
- Respects nerve. Anyone who surprises him gets remembered, and maybe an offer.

**Sample Lines:**
- "This doesn't need to be so difficult."
- "No ruckus tonight. Load them gently."
- "Nobody owns the sea. Some people just haven't been told yet."
- "Hush now. She's nearly asleep." *(about the lantern)*

**Seen in Play (Nerd Alert Campaign):**
- **Session 0:** Showed his scarred eye and "Black Talon" dagger. Lit the lantern. The bar slept.
- **Session 1:** Above deck. Scarlet brought him Oz's name.
- **Session 2 (planned):** At the wheel, turning the Talon toward the Emerald Dawn. Pleased with the new game.
