### **Scarlet**

\cols

![Scarlet]({{site.baseurl}}/images/NPCs/Scarlet.jpg "maxheight:210")

\col

**Ship:** [The Black Talon]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/Locations/Ships/TheBlackTalon/#the-black-talon)
**Ship Role:** Quartermaster
**Faction:** [The Black Fleet]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/Factions/TheBlackFleet/#the-black-fleet)
**Pronouns:** she/her

**General Appearance:**
- Neat in a way nobody else on the Talon is: buttoned coat, clean cuffs, hair pinned back
- A red sash, the only color aboard a black ship
- The **ledger** on a cord around her neck, worn soft at the corners
- Ink on her fingers. Always.

**Sounds Like:**
- Polite and patient. Says "please" and means it.
- Never raises her voice. Lets silence do the waiting for her.
- Asks a question, then writes down the answer, whatever it is

\endcols

**Defining Characteristic(s):**
- **Never bluffs.** If she says it will happen, it happens.
- Runs the Fleet day to day. Vane decides where it goes; Scarlet makes it work.
- Usually the Fleet's first face. If the Talon sends someone to talk, it's her.

**Motivation:**
- Keep the books balanced by the pirate code. Every share paid, fair to the copper.
- The ledger holds every debt ever owed to the Fleet, and who promised what
- Believes in the code more than in any captain, including Vane

**Flaw or Quirk:**
- Can't leave a debt unwritten or a promise unkept, even a bad one
- Very good at waiting. Too good. Sometimes the window closes while she's being patient.

**With the Party:**
- Interviews, never interrogates. Ledger open, one question underneath everything.
- Offers shares to anyone worth keeping. Shares, not wages.
- Remembers faces. Remembers who owes what.

**Sample Lines:**
- "Please, sit. This will go faster if we're both comfortable."
- "I don't bluff. I'd like you to remember that, so we can save time."
- "Everything aboard is shared by the code. Including what you just lost."
- "Take your time. I have all day. You have until dusk."

**Seen in Play (Nerd Alert Campaign):**
- **Session 0:** Led the shakedown at the Busted Maiden. Scooped the Waystone off the floor.
- **Session 1:** Interviewed Robyn, who pointed her at Maris. Fetched Maris's amulet and tarot deck for a reading, then locked her back up. Got **Oz's name** out of Finn, along with his offer to make an introduction if the Fleet let them all go. Ran up to tell Vane.
- **Session 2 (planned):** At the wheel with Vane, Waystone still at her hip. Finn's promise is in the ledger.
