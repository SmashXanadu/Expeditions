### **Finn**

\cols

![Finn]({{site.baseurl}}/images/NPCs/Finn.jpg "maxheight:210")

\col

**Ship:** [The Emerald Dawn]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/Locations/Ships/TheEmeraldDawn/#the-emerald-dawn)
**Ship Role:** Quartermaster and first mate
**Faction:** None
**Pronouns:** he/him

**General Appearance:**
- Lean, wiry, never fully still
- Sun-bleached brown hair escaping a crooked bandana
- Worn clothes, carefully patched
- Fingers always busy: a knot in a cord, a rolling coin, a loose thread
- Bright eyes that keep sliding to the water

**Sounds Like:**
- Rapid-fire, swinging between big laughs and conspiratorial whispers
- Thick, rolling accent as old as the sea
- Talks in nautical proverbs and omens

\endcols

**Defining Characteristic(s):**
- Boundless energy. Superstitious to the bone: every event, good or bad, is a sign.
- Uncanny knack for *finding* things: a missing tool, a forgotten snack, a trinket on the tide

**Motivation:**
- Reads every event as an omen and acts on the signs
- Collects odd artifacts and samples as lucky charms
- Quietly nudges the ship toward places that feel "blessed" or "cursed"

**Backstory:**
- Sailed with Wren on a previous crew. The two saved up enough gold to buy the Emerald Dawn and "decide their own fate for once."
- Was in the middle of hiring a crew when the Driftworks trouble hit. Oz and Pip were the first two he signed.

**Flaw or Quirk:**
- Terrified of calm seas. Stillness means something bad is coming.
- Mutters to himself when handling strange objects or staring out at the water
- A terrible fighter and not one to run toward a battle. Calls himself "the closest thing to a fighter this crew has," which says a lot about the crew.
- Afraid the Black Fleet will catch up to the ship. "The Black Fleet always remembers."

**With the Crew:**
- Trusts Wren implicitly. Calls her the brains of the operation.
- Does the hiring. Says yes to people on a good feeling and lets Wren sort out the rest.
- Everyone's friend by the second drink. Remembers names, birthdays, and lucky numbers.
- Reads omens into every new crewmate: what they carry, where they sit, what they order

**Sample Lines:**
- "Calm water's a held breath, friend. Something's always on the other end of it."
- "Fair shares, decent food, and a captain who doesn't shout. Mostly doesn't shout."
- "Ha! No. No, I like you. That's a good sign, that is. Sit, sit."
- "Can't leave a card face up on a tavern floor. Worst luck there is."
