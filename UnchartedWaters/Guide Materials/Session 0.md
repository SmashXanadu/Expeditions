## Micro Scenario: The Busted Maiden

**Players:** 3-5 | **Time:** 30 min | **Zones:** The Bar > The Floor > The Door

**Set the scene:**
- Signs all over the Markets: *"Crew wanted. Long haul, fair shares. Ask for Finn at the Busted Maiden."*
- Took an hour to find. It drifted two hulls east.
- Old merchant ship split bow to stern, right through the figurehead. Two halves lashed side by side, tavern built across the gap. Packed tonight.

**Outside:**
- **Looks:** Two half-hulls open like a book. Split laughing figurehead = the front door. Light through the seam.
- **Sounds:** Groaning rope, slapping water, fiddle, laughter when the door swings.
- **Smells:** Tar, wet rope, fried fish.
- **Feels:** Swaying gangway, spray.

### Zone 1: The Bar (Social, no rolls)
**Terrain:** Plank bar, hanging lamps, tilting floor

- **Looks:** Ribs curving up to a sailcloth roof (inside an opened whale). Bottles hung by the neck. Barkeep pulls water from the air.
- **Sounds:** Squeezebox, dice, a dozen accents, creaking hull.
- **Smells:** Rum, pipe smoke, lamp oil, old salt.
- **Feels:** Sticky, warm, close. Drinks slide on big swells.

**Ask each player:** *Why'd you answer the notice? Who are you sitting with? What are you doing right now?*

- **Finn** holds court in the corner. Interviews each PC. Hand on a belt pouch. Won't explain it.
- **Rumors:** Chatting up the room = d20 on Driftworks Rumors. Force #6 (Vane in the Lash Markets) if nobody rolls it.
- **Ratty the secret pirate:** Small, fidgety, same drink all night. **Observant TN 13:** Ratty's watching Finn's pouch, not the room.

#### Finn at a Glance (they/them)
- **Looks:** Wiry, never still. Crooked bandana, patched clothes. Fidgeting with cord, coin, thread. Keeps checking the porthole.
- **On the table:** Untouched tankard, shark tooth, green glass float, bored snail shell, short crew list.
- **Tonight:** Cheerful on top, jumpy underneath. Sea's been calm 6 days = bad omen. Laughs too loud. Startles at the door. Touches the pouch.
- **Interview = vibe check:**
  - "Worst storm you've been in? Not how bad. How'd it *feel*?"
  - Holds up the shell: "If I dropped this, would you pick it up or leave it?" (No wrong answer. Finn reads meaning into both.)
- **Lines:**
  - "Calm water's a held breath, friend. Something's always on the other end of it."
  - "Fair shares, decent food, and a captain who doesn't shout. Mostly doesn't shout."
  - "Ha! No. No, I like you. That's a good sign, that is. Sit, sit."
- **Earning the pouch:** Share a superstition, show a trinket, or notice Finn watching the water.
  - Whisper: "It's a Waystone. Ours. First one the ship's ever had. Wren trusted me to bring it aboard."
  - Blue glow through leather, tucked away fast.

**Advance when:** everyone's had a spotlight, or 12 min.

### Zone 2: The Floor (Social / Standoff)
**Terrain:** Tables, benches, cold iron stove, bilge trapdoor

- **Looks:** Mismatched wreck tables. Dead stove. Trapdoor half under a bench, brass ring.
- **Sounds:** Music dies mid-note. Chairs scrape, boots, bilge sloshing.
- **Smells:** Cold ash, then sea air and wet oilskin.
- **Feels:** Room goes cold. Everyone eyes the exits.

**The Fleet arrives:**
- Door bangs open. Six in black spread along the walls.
- Woman in front, ledger on a cord: *"Nobody's in trouble. We're just looking for something that was promised to us."*
- Sets a small black lantern on the bar. Unlit. *(Put the prop lantern down.)*

- **Finn:** Goes pale and quiet. Presses the pouch into the most trusted PC's hand under the table.
- **Scarlet** leads. Polite, orderly, not bluffing. Wants the Waystone, no mess.
- **Deckhands x4** block exits. Ratty joins them.

**Player options** (one action each, then advance):
- **Lie / stall / bargain:** Charismatic / Manipulator **TN 14**. Buys a round; Scarlet *likes* that PC.
- **Hide the Waystone:** Sneaky / Thief / Dexterous **TN 13**. Scarlet doesn't know who has it.
- **Read the crew:** Observant / Smart **TN 12**. The lantern's the threat, and a "captain" waits outside.
- **Bilge trapdoor:** Sneaky **TN 14**.
- **Fight:** Allowed. 1-2 rounds vs. Deckhands. Scarlet backs off, reaches for the lantern.
- **Push complications.** Scarlet remembers every one in Session 1.

**Advance when:** everyone's acted, a fight starts, or 12 min.

### Zone 3: The Door (Hazard, unwinnable)
**Terrain:** Split bow, fog pouring in

- **Looks:** Black night through the gap. Lamps shrink to pinpricks. Fog low like spilled milk.
- **Sounds:** Muffled. Voices flat, heartbeat loud, creaking slows to a lullaby.
- **Smells:** Snuffed candles, cold stone, overripe flowers.
- **Feels:** Heavy limbs, wet-canvas eyelids, curl-up cold.

**Vane arrives:**
- Tall figure, darkness obscured fog around face, unhurried, coat that drinks the light. No creak on the gangway.
- Scarlet steps aside. Shutter opens. Flame burns *black*. Fog pours out.
- *(LED on. Drop the music.)*

**The Black Lantern (Mag 15, whole room):** Everyone rolls **Resist TN 15**. Everyone sleeps anyway. The roll decides what they keep:

- **Fail by 6+:** Out cold. Nothing extra.
- **Fail by 1-5:** One last sentence of action.
- **Success:** One last action **+** one detail (the figure's face, *Black Talon* on a knife, fog bending *toward* the lantern).
- **Nat 20:** All that **+** a Session 1 Benefit (cell key ring, kept the Waystone, or Advantage on the first Resist vs. the lantern).

**Last line:** *"No ruckus tonight. Load them gently."*

**Cut to black.** No in-fiction answers. Write down every last action + detail.
