## Faction Relationships

<!-- vspace:5 --> 

**Enemies**
- **Gilded Union vs. Black Fleet:** the sea's defining war. Aurelia has a bounty on Vane's head; Vane has sunk three Union flagships in return.
- **Verdant Circle vs. Copper Coalition:** the Circle calls the Coalition's mining a slow murder of the ocean floor. Raids are escalating.
- **Black Fleet vs. Iron Legion:** the Legion's biggest paycheck is the Union's, which makes every Legion captain a target in Vane's eyes.

<!-- vspace:5 --> 

**Tensions**
- **Deep Crawlers vs. Tidal Conclave:** a quiet rivalry over who has the right to Old World relics. The Crawlers recover them, the Conclave believes they belong in its observatories.
- **Copper Coalition vs. Deep Crawlers:** they share the Fang at Rustreach Crag, and Bram suspects the Crawlers are skimming ore that belongs to his shafts.
- **Horizon Chasers vs. Verdant Circle:** every new chart Elara draws is, to the Circle, an invitation for someone else to come exploit what it protects.

<!-- vspace:5 --> 

**Allies**
- **Iron Legion & Gilded Union:** contracted muscle. Well-paid and loyal, for now.
- **Copper Coalition & Gilded Union:** the Coalition's ore is worthless without the Union's trade network to move it.
- **Deep Crawlers & Horizon Chasers:** kindred spirits, one exploring below the waterline and one above it. They trade charts and salvage rights freely.
- **Tidal Conclave & Verdant Circle:** a cautious alliance against unchecked exploitation, though they distrust each other's methods.
