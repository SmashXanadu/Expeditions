# [The World](#the-world)

The Uncharted Waters are not endless. A perpetual current carries ships in a long, safe circuit around the sea. Stray too far from it, and you'll run into the Fog: a wall of mist that has swallowed every ship that ever tested it. At the heart of the sea churns something vast, unknown, and dangerous enough to drive that current on its own.

Generations ago, catastrophe struck, severe enough to seal the sea behind the Fog and set that vast, nameless thing churning at its center. The specifics of what happened is lost to history. Only recently are ships daring to sail past the current their ancestors trusted with their lives.

Almost everyone here is born with a magical affinity: Fire, Water, Air, Earth, Light, Dark, Nature, Electric, or a supernatural gift for weapons and armor instead. Ships sail on wind magic, tools run up to early gunpowder, and airships exist but are rare enough to stop traffic in a harbor.

## How Sessions Work

**Uncharted Waters** is an **open table** campaign: no fixed group, no attendance requirement. Any mix of 2–5 players can set sail on a given session. Each session moves between two modes:

- **Expedition:** The crew sets off for a timed, self-contained adventure. Everything wraps up in one sitting, with no cliffhangers waiting on next week.
- **Downtime:** Between expeditions, play fast-forwards. Every player does all the **Standard Activities** and up to **2 Optional Activities**
    - **Standard Activities**: Gain Experience, Buy Perks, and Rest to full Health and Charges.
    - **Optional Activities**: Craft, Gather Information, Research, Work, or a Custom activity.

## What to Expect

- **Schedule:** We play every week, [Day] at [Time] for [Duration]. We only skip for emergencies or if fewer than two players sign up.
- **Evolving Rules:** The system's a work in progress. Your feedback helps shape it.
- **Evolving World:** We are creating and mapping the world for all future players of the setting. Make bold choices.
- **Sign Up:** Watch Discord for weekly game events and grab an open spot (2–5 players).
- **Bring a Character:** Use one you've already made, or bring an idea. Character creation is quick and we'll help.
- **Be Present:** Put distractions away. Your focus keeps the story moving for everyone.
- **Be Inclusive:** Welcome new players, listen to everyone's ideas, keep the table positive.
- **Help Share the Story:** We are building this story collaboratively. If you have an idea that makes for a cooler moment or bettery story please bring it up.
