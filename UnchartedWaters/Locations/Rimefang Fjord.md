### Rimefang Fjord

A narrow frozen inlet between two sheer black cliffs, its waterline fringed with jagged ice teeth. A whaling station of dark timber huts clings to the shore, serving the crews who hunt the frost-leviathans that winter in the deep channel.

- **The Vibe:** Stark, silent, and bitterly cold. Everything is black stone, white snow, and the red of fresh work on the ice.
- **The Hub:** **Harpoon Quay**, a long pier of salt-stiffened timber where whalers haul their catch to the cutting-floors and the Union factors haggle over the price of oil.
- **The Hook:** The leviathans have changed their route, and the Union's quotas have not. Hunters are pushing deeper into the fjord and the ice, and the Verdant Wake has been seen offshore, shadowing the whaling fleet.

--------
### Occupants

- **Union Whaling Factors:** Gilded Union agents who finance the hunts, set the prices, and keep the ledgers.
- **Harpooners:** Hard, patient hunters who take a share of each catch and trust nobody with a debt.
- **Ice-Cutters:** Crews who keep the channel open and the cutting-floors clear.
- **Wake Watchers:** A few Verdant Circle observers who document every kill and report to the Verdant Wake.

---------------
### Facilities

- **Harpoon Quay:** The main working pier, with winches and cutting-floors.
- **The Oil Sheds:** Long stone sheds where blubber is rendered and barrelled for the Union's ships.
- **The Frost Gate:** A chain of iron stakes and nets across the fjord mouth to trap leviathans in the channel.
