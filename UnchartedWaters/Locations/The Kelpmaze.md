### The Kelpmaze

Miles of towering gold-brown kelp floating in tangled walls, with narrow winding channels of open water between them. Sunlight slants down through the fronds, and anything that enters without a guide is likely to wander for days.

- **The Vibe:** Dim, green-gold, and tangled. Every turn looks the same, and every sound seems to come from the wrong direction.
- **The Hub:** **The Holdfast**, a village of rope and wood woven among the thickest stalks, where the Circle's kelp-wardens keep the only reliable charts of the safe channels.
- **The Hook:** The kelp is spreading and swallowing channels the Wardens used to keep open. Smugglers say the Circle is steering the growth on purpose to close a trade route, and the Black Fleet has started hiding ships in the oldest, deepest part of the maze.

--------
### Occupants

- **Kelp-Wardens:** Verdant Circle guides who tend the channels and track every shift in the maze.
- **Holdfast Weavers:** Rope-workers who build the walkways and buoys that mark the safe routes.
- **Lost Crews:** Sailors who stayed after losing their ships, and now work for the Wardens.
- **Black Fleet Smugglers:** Pirate crews who know a few hidden channels the Wardens do not.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Holdfast:** The village of woven platforms and huts.
- **Buoy Lines:** Chains of marker buoys that show the current safe channels, redrawn after every storm.
- **The Cutting Grounds:** Open pools where Wardens trim back the kelp.
