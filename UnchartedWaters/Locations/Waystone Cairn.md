### Waystone Cairn

A small tidal island scattered with ancient grey stones, some standing, some toppled, arranged in a loose ring that no chart can quite agree on. Lichen and sea grass cover them, and the island can only be reached across a causeway of wet sand at low tide.

- **The Vibe:** Quiet, ancient, and faintly expectant. Visitors tend to speak softly and stay close together.
- **The Hub:** **The Tripod Camp**, a surveyor's camp on the highest rock, where the Chasers record the position of every stone and argue about whether it has moved.
- **The Hook:** The stones are faintly warm to the touch, and the Chasers' compasses all point inward when they stand in the ring. Nobody can explain the draw, and the Chasers are not sure whether to chart it or leave it alone.

--------
### Occupants

- **Horizon Chasers:** Survey crews who measure, sketch, and re-measure every stone.
- **Causeway Guides:** Local boatmen who know the tides and charge accordingly.
- **Stone-Sitters:** Pilgrims and eccentrics who camp in the ring for days at a time.
- **Rival Collectors:** Treasure-seekers who want to chip pieces off the stones, and are not welcome.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Ring:** The main circle of standing and fallen stones.
- **The Tripod Camp:** The surveyors' tents and instruments.
- **The Causeway:** A strip of sand and rock that floods twice a day.
