### Cinderglass Dunes

A barren island of rolling black glass dunes and pale sand, glittering with shards that cut through boot leather. Half-buried war machines poke out of the drifts: a toppled siege tower, a rusted cannon barrel, a banner pole with no banner.

- **The Vibe:** Desolate, glittering, and dry. Heat shimmers off the glass and the wind sounds like breaking crystal.
- **The Hub:** **Fort Shard**, a small Legion outpost built from salvaged siege-works at the island's one stable rise.
- **The Hook:** The Legion treats the dunes as a proving ground and a salvage field, selling recovered weapons to anyone with coin. Some of the buried machines still work, and nobody knows who fought here or why.

--------
### Occupants

- **Iron Legion Salvage Crews:** Soldiers who dig, test, and catalogue the buried war machines.
- **Quartermaster Dray:** The outpost's quartermaster, who sells recovered gear and keeps strict accounts.
- **Glass-Cutters:** Independent workers who harvest the glittering shards for sale.
- **Treasure Hunters:** Freelancers who sneak past the Legion to dig for themselves.

---------------
### Facilities

- **Fort Shard:** The Legion outpost and storehouse.
- **The Proving Field:** A cleared stretch of dune where recovered weapons are tested.
- **The Boneyard:** A trench of half-buried war machines marked for salvage.
