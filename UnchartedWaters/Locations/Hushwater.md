### Hushwater

A stretch of sea so still it mirrors the sky. No wind, no swell, no gulls. Ships that enter slow to a stop, and the quiet is strong enough that people lower their voices without being told.

- **The Vibe:** Silent, glassy, and watchful. Voices carry too far and echoes arrive too late.
- **The Hub:** **The Drift Camp**, a loose raft of becalmed ships lashed together by crews who could not leave and have settled into waiting.
- **The Hook:** Something large turns slowly beneath the surface, and the calm spreads a little further every season. The Chasers have buoys at the edge, and they have stopped being able to say where the edge is.

--------
### Occupants

- **Becalmed Crews:** Sailors who have been stranded in the Drift Camp for months, some for years.
- **Horizon Chasers:** A survey team that measures the calm's boundary and the depth beneath it.
- **Oar-Runners:** Crews who row people out of the calm for a steep price.
- **Hushed Ones:** Residents who have stopped speaking, and nobody is sure why.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Drift Camp:** The raft of ships lashed together at the heart of the calm.
- **The Edge Buoys:** A line of chained buoys marking where the wind dies.
- **The Still Shore:** A thin strip of beach where rowers wait for passengers.
