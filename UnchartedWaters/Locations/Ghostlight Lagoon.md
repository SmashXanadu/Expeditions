### Ghostlight Lagoon

A calm lagoon ringed by pale sand, its water thick with glowing ghost-white jellyfish that pulse softly beneath the surface. At dusk the whole lagoon lights up like a field of slow stars.

- **The Vibe:** Serene, eerie, and beautiful. The glow makes everyone look a little pale, and conversation drops to a murmur.
- **The Hub:** **The Skimmer Racks**, a shoreline of wooden racks and copper vats where Coalition crews skim, press, and boil the jellies into lamp-fuel.
- **The Hook:** The lagoon's light fuels half the navies, and the Coalition is harvesting faster than the jellies can breed. Night crews report shapes moving in the glow, and the Verdant Circle has begun threatening to close the lagoon.

--------
### Occupants

- **Coalition Harvesters:** Copper Coalition crews who net the jellies and run the vats.
- **Lamp-Wrights:** Crafters who turn the glow into fuel and lantern cores.
- **Night-Watchers:** Guards and skimmers who work the lagoon after dark, and talk too little about what they see.
- **Verdant Observers:** A few Circle druids who document the harvest and warn of the consequences.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Skimmer Racks:** The harvest racks and press sheds.
- **The Copper Vats:** Rendering vats that boil the jellies into fuel.
- **The Lantern Net:** A glass-lantern net strung across the lagoon mouth to guide the jellies in.
