### Maw of the Deep One

The outer approach to the great whirlpool at the heart of the sea: a ring of black, jagged reef around a vast bottomless funnel of swirling water. Silver currents spiral inward, and the broken tips of ship masts sometimes surface and vanish again.

- **The Vibe:** Vast, grey, and dreadful. The noise is a low roar that never stops, and the horizon seems to tilt.
- **The Hub:** **The Last Mooring**, a chain-and-buoy station anchored to the outermost reef, where Crawler crews stage deep descents and say goodbye to anyone who goes too far.
- **The Hook:** Ships can only approach at slack water, and instruments behave strangely close in: compasses spin, depth-lines run out, and tethered divers report currents that pull against the whirlpool's spin. No one knows what the great whirlpool is or why it exists, and the Crawlers have a standing offer for anyone willing to go closer.

--------
### Occupants

- **Deep Crawlers:** Tether-crews who stage dives at the outer reef and carry out any survivors.
- **Mooring-Keepers:** A small crew who keep the Last Mooring's chains tended and its signal fires lit.
- **Reef-Runners:** Pilots who thread the outer reef for ships brave or stupid enough to come close.
- **Lost Sailors:** Survivors dragged out of the water, who rarely say what they saw.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Last Mooring:** A station of chains, buoys, and signal fires on the outer reef.
- **The Slack-Water Board:** A tide table that tells the crews when it is safe to approach.
- **The Reef Ring:** The black jagged reef that circles the whirlpool.
