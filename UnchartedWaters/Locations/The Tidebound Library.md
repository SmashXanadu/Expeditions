### The Tidebound Library

A sea cave opening at the waterline, its interior flooded to the shelves and kept dry above by a dome of trapped air held in place by old magic. Rows of books stand in water up to their spines, preserved by a shimmering curtain of bubbles.

- **The Vibe:** Hushed, damp, and scholarly. Lamplight reflects off the water, and every page turn echoes.
- **The Hub:** **The Reading Dock**, a floating platform at the cave mouth where visitors are met, questioned, and handed a lamp and a boat.
- **The Hook:** The air-dome is failing. Every season a few more shelves slip under, and the Conclave has begun deciding which books to save. The Gilded Union has offered to buy the whole collection, and the Crawlers have been selling books they pulled up from the deep.

--------
### Occupants

- **Conclave Archivists:** Tidal Conclave scholars who catalogue, repair, and guard the collection.
- **Boat-Librarians:** Staff who pole visitors between shelves and fetch whatever they ask for.
- **Union Appraisers:** Gilded Union agents who want to buy, copy, or simply count the books.
- **Crawler Dealers:** Deep Crawlers who sell salvaged books to the highest bidder.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Reading Dock:** The entrance platform and front desk.
- **The Dry Stacks:** The highest shelves, still above the water, holding the most valuable works.
- **The Bubble Curtain:** The shimmering wall of trapped air that keeps the dome in place.
