### Vinewreck

A graveyard of old ships tangled and fused into one floating island, overgrown with vines and magenta orchids. Masts stand like trees, and rope bridges link hulls that have long since forgotten they were separate vessels.

- **The Vibe:** Wild, lush, and overgrown. Everything creaks softly, and the smell is of wet wood and flowers.
- **The Hub:** **The Garden Deck**, the flattest and most overgrown deck of the largest hull, where the Circle's gardeners tend rare plants and receive visitors.
- **The Hook:** The wreck is growing, drawing in drifting ships and wrapping them in vines. Black Fleet salvagers want to strip the holds, many of which still contain intact cargo, and the Circle wants the wreck left alone.

--------
### Occupants

- **Circle Gardeners:** Verdant Circle druids who tend the living wreck and prune its vines.
- **Hull-Keepers:** Residents who live in the oldest hulls and know which ones are safe.
- **Black Fleet Salvagers:** Pirates who raid the wreck for cargo under cover of night.
- **Orchid Collectors:** Traders who pay well for the rarest flowers.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Garden Deck:** The main gathering place and plant nursery.
- **The Rope Bridges:** The web of bridges linking the hulls.
- **The Sealed Holds:** Cargo holds overgrown with roots, some still sealed from the outside.
