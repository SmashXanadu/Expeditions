### Glassdeep

A sunken city of columns, domes, and wide streets lying on the sea floor in water so clear it can be seen from the deck of a passing ship. It sits in shallows just deep enough that no diver can reach it without a bell.

- **The Vibe:** Wondrous, eerie, and hushed. Light falls in slow shafts through the water onto streets no one has walked in a very long time.
- **The Hub:** **Bell Landing**, a cluster of moored ships and floating work-decks above the city where diving bells are lowered and hauled back up.
- **The Hook:** Everyone can see Glassdeep, which means everyone wants it. The Crawlers say it is theirs, the Union has filed a salvage claim on every dome, and the city's streets have been quietly changing since the first bells went down.

--------
### Occupants

- **Deep Crawlers:** Bell-divers who map the streets and haul up whatever the city yields.
- **Union Claim-Agents:** Gilded Union clerks who record every find and dispute every share.
- **Tidal Conclave Observers:** A few scholars who dive for the sake of study, not profit.
- **Rival Salvagers:** Independent crews who sneak in at dusk and dive without a license.

---------------
### Facilities

- **Bell Landing:** The surface platforms and moorings, always busy.
- **The Drowned Streets:** The ruins themselves, reachable only by bell.
- **The Claim House:** A floating office where Union clerks sort out who owns what.
