### The Carrion Court

A tall sandstone sea-cliff pocked with ledges and nests, home to hundreds of black birds that wheel and cry around it day and night. Carved balconies and a pirate banner are set into the rock face, with a rope ladder leading down to a hidden cove.

- **The Vibe:** Sinister, noisy, and watchful. Feathers drift down like ash and every shadow seems to have an eye.
- **The Hub:** **The Roost**, a wide balcony carved into the cliff where Black Fleet captains hold court and the bird-keepers sell their wares.
- **The Hook:** The birds are the Black Fleet's eyes, carrying messages and watching the sea lanes. A bird-keeper has begun selling the same birds to other buyers, and the Fleet does not yet know.

--------
### Occupants

- **Black Fleet Captains:** Pirate leaders who use the cliff as a hidden base and listening post.
- **Bird-Keepers:** Handlers who raise, train, and sell messenger and spy birds.
- **Cliff-Climbers:** Scouts and guards who patrol the ledges and ropes.
- **Cove Smugglers:** Crews who use the hidden cove for unloading cargo unseen.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Roost:** The main balcony and meeting place.
- **The Aviaries:** Rows of cages and nests built into the cliff.
- **The Hidden Cove:** A narrow cove, reachable only by rope ladder or low-tide boat.
