### Amberfall Grove

A cliffside forest of giant trees that weep glowing amber sap down their trunks. Great translucent nuggets collect at their roots, and ancient insects and small birds are visible inside them, caught in poses of surprise.

- **The Vibe:** Warm, golden, and still. The air smells of resin and the light has a honey color even at noon.
- **The Hub:** **The Sap Stair**, a wooden stairway up the cliff with tapping platforms along its length, where the Circle's gardeners harvest what the trees give freely.
- **The Hook:** The Gilded Union pays a fortune for large amber pieces with creatures inside, and independent tappers have begun cutting the trees to get at them. The Circle treats the grove as sacred and has begun to answer in kind.

--------
### Occupants

- **Circle Tappers:** Verdant Circle druids who harvest sap gently and tend the trees.
- **Amber-Wrights:** Craftspeople who carve and polish the nuggets for trade.
- **Union Buyers:** Gilded Union agents who bid for the best specimens.
- **Poachers:** Independent crews who cut trees for quick profit.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Sap Stair:** The cliffside stairway and tapping platforms.
- **The Amber Hall:** A workshop where nuggets are cut, sorted, and displayed.
- **The Heart Tree:** The oldest and largest tree in the grove, ringed with offerings.
