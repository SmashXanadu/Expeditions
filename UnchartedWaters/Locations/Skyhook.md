### Skyhook

A scattering of rock islands hanging in the air above the sea, each held to the water by thick bronze chains that run down into the deep. Thin waterfalls pour off their edges, and the largest island carries a small ruined arch.

- **The Vibe:** Airy, ancient, and slightly unreal. The islands sway on their chains, and the wind sings in the links.
- **The Hub:** **Anchor Camp**, a surveyor's settlement of tents and rope bridges on the largest island, run by the Horizon Chasers.
- **The Hook:** No two charts agree on how many islands there are, because they shift on their chains. One island has pulled loose and is drifting, and nobody knows what holds the others up, or what is at the other end of the chains.

--------
### Occupants

- **Horizon Chasers:** Surveyors and cartographers who climb the chains, map the islands, and argue about the count.
- **Chain-Climbers:** Daredevil guides who lead visitors up the links for a fee.
- **Island Hermits:** A few settlers who farm the thin soil on the smaller rocks.
- **Rival Collectors:** Treasure-hunters who want whatever is on the largest island.

---------------
### Facilities

- **Anchor Camp:** The surveyor settlement on the largest rock.
- **The Chain Stairs:** The climbable chains that connect the islands to the sea.
- **The Ruined Arch:** A weathered stone arch on the largest island, studied but unexplained.
