### Hollow Crown

The half-sunk skull of something enormous, the size of a hill, rising out of the sea with its jaw submerged. Lanterns glow in its eye sockets, and its great teeth have been fitted with planks and mooring rings to make a row of docks.

- **The Vibe:** Theatrical, ominous, and a little proud of itself. Every pirate who passes puts on a show.
- **The Hub:** **The Tooth Docks**, a row of tooth-docks where Black Fleet ships moor, with the throne room carved into the skull's brain-cavity above.
- **The Hook:** Black Fleet captains meet here to settle disputes under the skull's eye. A Union informant has been feeding the Gilded Union their plans, and every captain suspects every other.

--------
### Occupants

- **Black Fleet Captains:** Pirate lords who meet in the skull to divide spoils and settle grudges.
- **The Crown-Keeper:** A veteran who runs the skull's day-to-day affairs, and keeps a ledger of every favor owed.
- **Tooth-Dockers:** Sailors and scavengers who maintain the docks and sell supplies to arriving ships.
- **Union Informants:** At least one hidden spy among the crews.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Tooth Docks:** A row of mooring posts built into the skull's teeth.
- **The Eye Lamps:** Lantern-filled eye sockets that serve as beacon and watchtower.
- **The Hollow Throne:** A carved meeting hall in the skull where captains hold council.
