### The Driftworks

A colossal armada of ships lashed together by rope, chain, and makeshift platforms, drifting as one across the open sea.

- **The Vibe:** Vibrant, crowded, resourceful.
- **The Hub:** The **Grand Flotilla**, a core of heavy iron-clad hulls that anchor the city. Council of Captains, main foundries, the Great Winch.
- **The Route:** Locked to the world's current. Follows a slow, predictable circle.
- **The Hook:** The layout never sits still. Ropes get retied with the tide, and the tavern next to your lodgings this morning can be three blocks away by sunset. Getting lost is normal. Directions cost money.

**Senses:**
- **Looks:** Forest of masts and laundry lines. Signs pointing to places that have moved. Lamps strung through the rigging.
- **Sounds:** Constant creaking, like the city is breathing. Forge hammers, gulls, hawkers, the slow clank of the Great Winch.
- **Smells:** Tar, salt, forge smoke, frying fish, wet rope.
- **Feels:** Nothing is level. Decks rise and fall out of rhythm. Newcomers walk like they've had a few.

---------
### Occupants

- **Harbormaster Coil:** Sharp-eyed veteran. Tracks ship movements, decides where new ships tie on, knows where everything drifted (for a price).
- **The Council of Captains:** Rotating assembly of shipmasters. Power weighted by how many hulls each controls.
- **Scrap-Kin Crews:** Salvager clans on the lower decks. Harvest wreckage, patch the supports that hold the city together.
- **Black Fleet Agents:** Pirates under false colors. Fence stolen goods, vanish into the shifting layout.
- **Horizon Chasers Outpost:** A standing chart-room aboard one hull. Mobile base for mapping expeditions.

------------
### Facilities

- **The Great Winch:** Colossal, groaning crank. Hauls in wrecks, derelicts, and new ships.
- **The Lash Markets:** Stalls on every flat surface. Rope, chain, timber, salvage, fish, and things nobody admits stealing.
- **Drywake Forges:** Foundries in ironclad hulls. Scrap reforged into tools, weapons, and hull plates.
