### The Busted Maiden

*A tavern in [The Driftworks]({{site.baseurl}}/UnchartedWaters/Locations/Driftworks/#the-driftworks)*

She was a merchant ship once, the *Laughing Maiden*. Then something split her clean in two, bow to stern, straight through her figurehead. Nobody agrees on what. Rather than let her sink, someone hauled both halves into the Driftworks, lashed them side by side, and built a tavern across the break.

- **The Vibe:** Loud, warm, lucky. A ship that refused to sink.
- **Finding It:** It drifts loose constantly. Regulars ask, "Where's the Maiden tonight?"
- **Why Finn Chose It:** A ship split in two that refused to sink is the best possible omen for recruiting a crew.

**Senses:**
- **Looks:** Two half-hulls open like a book. Ribs curving up to a patched sailcloth roof, like the inside of an opened whale. Bottles hung by the neck from the rafters.
- **Sounds:** A fiddle or a wheezing squeezebox, dice on wood, a dozen accents, the hull creaking with the swell.
- **Smells:** Spilled rum, pipe smoke, lamp oil, old salt soaked deep into the wood.
- **Feels:** Sticky, warm, close. The floor tilts. Drinks slide toward the gap.

---------
### Layout

Two upright half-hulls, a body-length apart. Planks and grating bridge the gap down the middle of the room, with black water sloshing underneath.

- **The Bow (front door):** The two halves of the carved Maiden face each other across the entrance, each with half a smile.
- **Port Half (the bar):** A long plank counter along the inside of the hull, lamps overhead.
- **Starboard Half (the floor):** Mismatched tables salvaged from a dozen wrecks, a cold iron stove nobody has lit in years, and a bilge trapdoor half hidden under a bench.

------------
### Occupants

- **The Barkeep:** A water mage who refills the washbasin and the cheap ale straight out of the air. *(Unnamed for now. Name them in play if a player asks.)*
- **Regulars:** Sailors between ships, Lash Markets traders, and anyone who wants a drink nobody will ask questions about.

------------
### House Quirks

- The two halves don't rock in time. Regulars sit with a hand on their mug.
- It's lucky to touch both halves of the Maiden's face on the way in. Finn always does, and gets visibly upset if he forgets.
