### Bonecoil Isle

A jungle island where giant fossilized ribs arch like bridges over the trees. A village has grown inside the largest rib cage, and the shoreline is scarred by quarries cut into pale bone.

- **The Vibe:** Primeval, dusty, and strangely quiet. Wind whistles through the ribs like a distant chorus.
- **The Hub:** **The Ribhouse**, a town built under the great rib cage, its roofs and walls made of salvaged bone and canvas.
- **The Hook:** Coalition metallurgists have found that powdered bone makes the strongest alloy yet, and the quarry has uncovered a spine far larger than anything anyone has seen. The quarry foreman wants to keep digging, and the locals say the island has been growing.

--------
### Occupants

- **Coalition Quarrymen:** Copper Coalition crews who cut, haul, and grind the bone.
- **Bone-Smelters:** Furnace crews who turn powdered bone into alloy for the navies.
- **Ribhouse Residents:** Settlers and guides who lived here before the quarry and resent it.
- **Crawler Prospectors:** A few Deep Crawlers who claim the bones belong to the sea floor, not the shore.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Ribhouse:** The main settlement under the largest rib cage.
- **The Bone Quarry:** Open cuts and scaffolds along the pale shoreline.
- **The Grinding Sheds:** Mills that turn raw bone into powder for the smelters.
