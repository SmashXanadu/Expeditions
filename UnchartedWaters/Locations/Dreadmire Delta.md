### Dreadmire Delta

A river-mouth swamp of twisted mangroves and murky brown water, where ramshackle stilt-houses are joined by rope walkways and pale green mist clings to the surface even at noon.

- **The Vibe:** Humid, rotten, and feverish. Everything is damp, and the buzzing never stops.
- **The Hub:** **Stilt Row**, a crooked street of stilt-houses and taverns where smugglers, fences, and fugitives trade and hide.
- **The Hook:** A fever runs through the delta and gives its victims vivid dreams of a vast, dark water. The Black Fleet uses the sickness as cover for its smuggling. The Verdant Circle calls the delta's water dead and wants to cleanse it.

--------
### Occupants

- **Black Fleet Smugglers:** Pirate crews who use the delta as a staging ground and fence their goods through Stilt Row.
- **Mire-Doctors:** Independent healers who treat the fever with herbs and secrecy.
- **The Feverish:** Residents who have survived the sickness and describe the same dreams.
- **Verdant Cleansers:** Circle druids who work to purify the water, and are not welcome.

---------------
### Facilities

- **Stilt Row:** The main street of stilt-houses, shops, and taverns.
- **The Fever Ward:** A raised hospital hut where the sick are kept apart.
- **Smugglers' Channels:** A web of hidden waterways through the mangroves.
