### Fogwall Watch

A lone stone fortress on a rock at the edge of the Great Fog, a towering wall of grey-white that rolls slowly outward over the water. A single red beacon burns from the top of the keep, and the walls are manned day and night.

- **The Vibe:** Isolated, tense, and damp. Sound dies in the fog and the soldiers talk in low voices.
- **The Hub:** **Gate Keep**, the fortress's central courtyard, where the Legion logs arrivals and keeps a ledger of everything that drifts out of the Fog.
- **The Hook:** The Fog is a physical fact, and no one knows what causes it. Wreckage, strange cargo, and occasionally living survivors drift out of it, and the Legion records every one and shares little with outsiders. The fog has moved a little closer to the fort each year.

--------
### Occupants

- **Iron Legion Garrison:** Disciplined soldiers who watch the fog line and answer to the Commander's staff.
- **The Watch-Captain:** The fortress's commander, a tired veteran who keeps the logs and the secrets.
- **Drift-Collectors:** Crews who gather what floats out of the Fog and bring it to the fort.
- **Outcast Survivors:** People who emerged from the Fog and are held under watch.

---------------
### Facilities

- **Gate Keep:** The courtyard and log-house at the heart of the fortress.
- **The Red Beacon:** The warning fire at the top of the keep.
- **The Drift Pier:** A stone pier where salvage and survivors are brought ashore.
