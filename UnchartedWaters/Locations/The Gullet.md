### The Gullet

A churning whirlpool a few hundred paces across that has sat in the same patch of open sea for as long as anyone remembers. Along its inner wall, a spiral stair of drowned stone ruins winds down into the dark, exposed in stretches as the water rises and falls.

- **The Vibe:** Roaring, dizzying, and hypnotic. Spray hangs in the air and the sea hums under every hull.
- **The Hub:** **Rimwatch**, a ring of rope-lashed platforms on the calm outer edge, where Crawler bell-crews stage their dives and ships hold position at a respectful distance.
- **The Hook:** The Gullet is only a local whirlpool, nothing to do with the great one at the heart of the sea, but it is slowly widening. Each month a new landing of the stair surfaces, and the Crawlers are racing to reach the bottom before the water takes it back.

--------
### Occupants

- **Deep Crawlers:** Bell-divers and tether-crews who treat the stair as the best salvage site in the western sea.
- **Bell-Masters:** Crew chiefs who read the spin of the water before every descent and decide who goes down.
- **Rimwatch Pilots:** Local sailors who guide ships around the edge for a fee.
- **Union Assessors:** Gilded Union buyers who wait on the platforms to bid on whatever comes up.

---------------
### Facilities

- **Rimwatch Platforms:** Floating docks and bunkhouses held against the current by heavy chain.
- **Bell Cradles:** Great brass diving bells on gantries, tended day and night.
- **The Stair:** Sections of ruined steps winding down the whirlpool wall, reachable only during the lulls.
