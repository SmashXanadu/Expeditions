### The Pale Wardens

Five crumbling chalk-white lighthouses standing in a ring on a rocky shoal. Each tower is crowned with a cold silver flame that burns in all weather, and together the five mark the safest channel through the reef.

- **The Vibe:** Watchful, haunting, and cold. The flames give no heat, and the towers seem to turn toward whoever approaches.
- **The Hub:** **Warden's Rest**, a squat stone barracks on the shoal's centre, where the Legion garrison rotates between towers.
- **The Hook:** One tower has gone dark, and the garrison that held it has not been seen since. The Legion says it is a supply problem. The silver flames of the other four burn a little brighter every night.

--------
### Occupants

- **Iron Legion Garrison:** Disciplined soldiers who man the towers and keep the channel clear for friendly ships.
- **Tower-Sergeants:** Veterans in charge of each beacon, who log every ship that passes.
- **Channel Pilots:** Locals who guide ships through the reef for a fee, and do not like the dark tower.
- **Wandering Scholars:** A few outsiders who want to study the silver flames, and are watched closely.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Five Towers:** Chalk-white lighthouses with cold silver beacons.
- **Warden's Rest:** The central barracks and mess.
- **The Dark Tower:** The fifth tower, silent and unlit, with its door barred from the outside.
