### The Singing Reef

A razor-sharp coral reef shaped like organ pipes, ringed by turquoise water. The wind and tides pass through the hollow coral and produce a slow, shifting chord that carries for miles.

- **The Vibe:** Beautiful, hypnotic, and treacherous. The song sounds like home to everyone who hears it.
- **The Hub:** **The Tuning Station**, a platform of stone and brass on the reef's calm side, where Conclave scholars study the song and sand down the pipes.
- **The Hook:** The song changes with the tides, and sailors steer toward it without knowing why, tearing their hulls open on the coral. The Conclave tries to damp the worst notes, and a few ships' crews have started paying to hear the reef at its loudest.

--------
### Occupants

- **Conclave Tuners:** Tidal Conclave scholars who study the song and try to shape it.
- **Reef-Wardens:** Guards who turn ships back and rescue the crews who ignore them.
- **Song-Chasers:** Visitors who sail close, plug their ears, and record what they hear.
- **Wreck-Pickers:** Scavengers who work the reef's edge for the cargo of ships that came too close.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Tuning Station:** The Conclave's observation platform.
- **The Pipe Fields:** The largest clusters of organ-pipe coral.
- **The Warning Buoys:** A ring of floating bells that sound when ships drift too near.
