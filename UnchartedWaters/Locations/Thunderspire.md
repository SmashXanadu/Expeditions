### Thunderspire

A needle of black rock rising from a perpetual storm, struck by lightning so often that the air around it smells of scorched metal. The Coalition has crowned its upper reaches with copper coils, rods, and glass jars to catch the strikes.

- **The Vibe:** Electric, deafening, and hair-raising. Thunder never stops and the sea around the spire is white with rain.
- **The Hub:** **The Coil**, a ring of copper capacitors and platforms near the summit where storm-catchers bottle the lightning.
- **The Hook:** The storms are growing stronger, and the Coalition has begun overcharging the spire to keep up with demand. Tidal Conclave observers have set up camp nearby and claim that every bolt taken from the sky disturbs the tides for miles.

--------
### Occupants

- **Storm-Catchers:** Copper Coalition crews who tend the coils and risk their lives swapping jars between strikes.
- **Foreman Voss:** A burn-scarred foreman who rations lightning like ore and pays for results, not caution.
- **Tidal Conclave Watchers:** A small camp of scholars who record the spire's effect on the sea and want it shut down.
- **Spark-Runners:** Couriers who carry charged jars to Coalition ships, paid by the jar.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Coil:** The summit platform of copper and glass.
- **The Jar Racks:** Rows of charged glass jars stored in rubber-lined sheds.
- **The Climb:** A chain-and-rung ladder up the spire, slick and dangerous in any weather.
