### The Hollow Berg

A mountain of drifting ice with a great arched cave in its flank. Inside, columns of blue ice rise into a vaulted nave that glows faintly from within, shaped and tended over generations into something between a cloister and an observatory.

- **The Vibe:** Quiet, reverent, and cold enough to hurt. Every footstep rings back from the ice.
- **The Hub:** **The Nave**, the central hall, where Conclave scholars hold their vigils and read the tides through slabs of clear ice cut into lenses.
- **The Hook:** The berg is drifting off its old circuit toward warmer water. If it keeps going, the lower galleries will melt through, including a sealed gallery that no one has opened in living memory.

--------
### Occupants

- **Conclave Hermits:** Tidal Conclave scholars who live in the ice for years at a time, studying the pull of the tides.
- **Lens-Cutters:** Craftspeople who shape the observatory ice into lenses and mirrors.
- **Berg Pilots:** A small sailing crew that tows and steers the berg as best they can.
- **Wandering Petitioners:** Visitors who climb the ice stair to ask the Conclave for guidance.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Nave:** A cathedral of blue ice used for study and ceremony.
- **The Ice Stair:** A long switchback of cut steps from the waterline to the cave mouth.
- **The Sealed Gallery:** A deep chamber blocked with a wall of solid ice, marked and watched.
