### Tallow Harbor

A whale-rendering port of tall white bone-towers and smokestacks, with racks of enormous ribs along the shore and stacks of oil barrels on every pier. Greasy grey smoke hangs over the water, and the stench drives off all but the desperate.

- **The Vibe:** Grimy, busy, and foul. Everyone works fast and nobody lingers.
- **The Hub:** **The Rendery**, a vast open shed of copper vats and bone racks where the Union's crews boil down catches from across the sea.
- **The Hook:** Tallow Harbor supplies lamp-oil and candles for half the western sea, and the Union holds the supply chain tight. The Verdant Circle protests loudly at the gates, and some of the workers have started to listen.

--------
### Occupants

- **Union Rendering Crews:** Gilded Union laborers who cut, boil, and barrel the catch.
- **The Harbor Factor:** The Union's local agent, who sets prices and manages the ledgers.
- **Oil-Runners:** Couriers who ferry barrels to waiting ships.
- **Verdant Protesters:** A camp of Circle activists who chain themselves to the piers.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Rendery:** The main rendering shed.
- **Rib Racks:** Long frames of drying bone, used for building and trade.
- **The Barrel Yard:** A huge open area of stacked oil casks awaiting shipment.
