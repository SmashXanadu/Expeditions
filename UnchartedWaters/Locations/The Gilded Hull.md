### The Gilded Hull

A treasure galleon with a gold-leaf hull, wedged on a reef long ago and since converted into a floating casino. Strings of lanterns and striped awnings cover the deck, and a faint translucent figure still stands at the helm.

- **The Vibe:** Decadent, glittering, and just slightly haunted. The music is a little too slow and the dealers never blink.
- **The Hub:** **The Quarterdeck Floor**, the main gaming deck, where the Union's house-dealers run games and the ghost-captain watches every hand.
- **The Hook:** The ghost-captain wants a debt repaid, and anyone who settles it wins their freedom from the Hull's tables. The Gilded Union runs the house, and the Black Fleet lurks in the lower decks for a chance at the vault.

--------
### Occupants

- **The Ghost-Captain:** The translucent former master of the galleon, who stands at the helm and forgets nothing.
- **Union House-Dealers:** Gilded Union staff who run the games and keep the books.
- **High-Rollers:** Merchants, pirates, and gamblers drawn by the stakes.
- **Black Fleet Infiltrators:** Pirates posing as patrons, probing the vault.

---------------
### Facilities

- **The Quarterdeck Floor:** The main casino and gaming deck.
- **The Captain's Table:** A private card room at the stern, guarded and reserved.
- **The Vault:** A locked hold below the waterline, said to hold the galleon's original treasure.
